# ECE-121-Lab-Reports
ECE 121 is a circuits laboratory course focused on the analysis, simulation, and implementation of electronic circuits. The course emphasizes predicting circuit behavior through analytical calculations and Cadence PSpice simulations prior to physical implementation. Circuits were constructed and tested on breadboards, with measured results compared against theoretical and simulated values. Standard laboratory instrumentation such as oscilloscopes, multimeters, and power supplies were used to validate circuit performance. The work in this repository demonstrates applied circuit analysis, simulation-driven design, and experimental verification skills.
